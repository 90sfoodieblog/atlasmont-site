The Currents Around the Metro

In Richmond, the present era's currents run in several directions at once: toward the South and its suburbs, out of the highest-cost coastal metros, and into mid-sized metros whose Black populations are growing fastest in percentage terms. The Richmond metro's record, in the published counts, is its own negotiation among those currents.
