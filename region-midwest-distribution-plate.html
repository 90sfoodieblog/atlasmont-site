Black America in the Midwest: The Distribution Plate

The Midwest is one of the four great regions of the federal statistical system, and its Black population — about 8.3 million residents, alone or in combination, roughly 15.7% of the national total of 52.8 million — gives it a distinct place in every national table. This plate reads the region's Black distribution as a single geography, state by state and then whole.

The Region State by State

The region's largest Black populations are held by Illinois (2 million), Ohio (1.6 million), Michigan (1.6 million), Missouri (785,000), Indiana (750,000). The order matters: regional totals are not abstractions but sums of these state communities, and a change in any one of them moves the region. The state plates of this atlas carry each of these populations in full; this plate's work is the aggregation — the region as its residents actually compose it.
