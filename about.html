About Atlasmont

Contact: info@blackamericadata.com · Advertising: advertising@blackamericadata.com

Atlasmont is The Black America Atlas — the atlas property of the Black America Data reference family. Where the core reference answers in articles, Atlasmont answers in every measure mapped, tabled, and tied to the published federal or institutional source it came from.

The atlas is organized by place — nation, region, state, metro, county — and by the measures that describe a place: distribution, income, education, health, business, and movement. Its are timeless by design: built from the decennial census and the American Community Survey, they describe the standing geography of Black America rather than the news of a season.

Atlasmont — an Atlas reference. All figures are drawn from published federal and institutional sources, named on each page.
