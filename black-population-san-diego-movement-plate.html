The Currents Around the Metro

In San Diego, the present era's currents run in several directions at once: toward the South and its suburbs, out of the highest-cost coastal metros, and into mid-sized metros whose Black populations are growing fastest in percentage terms. The San Diego metro's record, in the published counts, is its own negotiation among those currents. In San Diego, each decennial census and each survey year revises the picture at the margin; the direction of the lines is the story, and this is built to be re-read as the lines extend.
